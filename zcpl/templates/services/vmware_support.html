Major Increase in VMware License Spend

Broadcom ended perpetual license renewals and restructured VMware software into mandatory VVF or VCF bundles. Organisations report VMware license cost increases of more than 2× their prior annual spend.

📦

Forced Product Bundling

VMware technical support under Broadcom now requires purchasing VMware vSphere Foundation (VVF) or VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF), a bundled stack that includes VMware products many organisations do not need and did not budget for.

🔒

Perpetual Licences Left Without Coverage

Broadcom no longer offers support renewals for perpetual VMware licences. Organisations that paid for and rely on VMware vSphere environments are now left without OEM-backed VMware technical support.

👥

Internal Skills Shortage

Managing VMware ESXi hosts, vCenter, DRS, Distributed Switch and HA configurations in-house requires deep expertise. Building and retaining that capability internally is costly, particularly when the environment is already stable and meeting operational needs.
