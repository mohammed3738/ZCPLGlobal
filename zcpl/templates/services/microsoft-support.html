Why Organisations Are Reconsidering Their Microsoft Support Costs

Microsoft's Fixed Lifecycle Policy runs on a fixed commercial schedule. Once a product version crosses its end-of-life date, Microsoft withdraws all security patches, bug fixes, and technical support, regardless of whether the software is stable, embedded in critical operations, or simply not yet ready for replacement.

Organisations report their annual microsoft support costs increasing year on year without any corresponding increase in the number of incidents raised or the quality of support received.

Understanding the Lifecycle

How Microsoft's Fixed Lifecycle Policy Works, and Where the Support Gap Occurs

Microsoft's Fixed Lifecycle Policy provides a structured 10-year support window for Business and Developer products such as SQL Server and Windows Server. This is divided into five years of Mainstream Support (full feature updates, security patches, design change requests, and free incident support) followed by five years of Extended Support (security patches and paid incident support only).

When Extended Support ends, the product reaches End-of-Life. Microsoft stops all updates, bug fixes, and technical support. The only OEM option available post-EOL is the purchase of Extended Security Updates (ESUs) for up to three years, but according to Microsoft's own documentation, ESUs cover only critical and important security patches. They explicitly do not include non-security hotfixes, incident support, or configuration guidance. This is the operational gap that our microsoft it support fills.

Source: learn.microsoft.com, Extended Security Updates for Windows Server overview (what ESUs do and do not include) Source: learn.microsoft.com, Product Lifecycle FAQ: Extended Security Updates

Our microsoft managed services provides full incident resolution, active vulnerability management, and technical advisory across all lifecycle stages, including environments well past Microsoft's official EOL date. The migration schedule is set by your organisation's requirements, not by Microsoft's product calendar.

Mainstream Support: Years 1 to 5

Full OEM coverage: new feature development, security updates, design changes, and free incident support. Microsoft is fully engaged during this phase.

Extended Support: Years 6 to 10

Security patches and paid incident support only. No new features or design changes. Many enterprises are still running these versions when the EOL date arrives.

End-of-Life, All OEM Support Stops

All Microsoft patches and technical support cease. ESUs available for up to 3 further years, critical security patches only, no incident support or bug fixes. Windows Server 2012 R2, SQL Server 2012, and SQL Server 2014 are at this stage.

What is Microsoft third-party support and how does it differ from Unified Support?

Microsoft third party support means engaging an independent provider for break/fix incident resolution, security vulnerability management, and technical guidance across your Microsoft infrastructure, rather than purchasing support directly from Microsoft.

The core differences are pricing model and coverage scope. Microsoft Unified Support is priced as a percentage of your total annual Microsoft spend, with a minimum annual contract of $50,000 for the Core tier (as reported by Directions on Microsoft: directionsonmicrosoft.com). Our microsoft support is scoped to your actual environment, pricing reflects the systems you need covered, not your entire Microsoft spend.

What does windows server 2012 r2 end of life mean for our environment?

Windows server 2012 r2 end of life arrived on 10 October 2023, as confirmed by Microsoft's official lifecycle documentation (learn.microsoft.com). From that date, Microsoft no longer issues security patches, non-security hotfixes, bug fixes, or technical support for the product. Any vulnerability discovered after that date remains unpatched by the OEM indefinitely.

Microsoft's Extended Security Updates (ESUs) offer up to three further years of critical security patches only. They do not include incident support or configuration guidance. Our microsoft it support provides the full scope: active vulnerability management, break/fix incident resolution, and technical advisory, enabling your organisation to remain protected while a migration is planned at a controlled pace.
