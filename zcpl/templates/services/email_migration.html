Common Questions

Email Migration FAQ

1. What is email migration?
Email migration is the process of transferring emails, contacts, and calendars from one platform to another. Businesses use email migration services to move to cloud platforms like Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or Zoho for better performance and accessibility.
2. How long does email migration take?
The duration depends on users, mailbox size, and platforms. Most email migration services are completed within a few days. A proper assessment helps define timelines and ensures minimal disruption during the migration process.
3. Will there be downtime during migration?
Our email migration services are designed to reduce downtime. In some cases, users can continue working during the migration. We follow a structured approach to maintain business continuity while transitioning to the new platform.
4. Is my data secure during migration?
Yes, we follow secure email migration practices including backups and controlled access. Data is protected at every stage to ensure accuracy throughout the migration process.
5. What platforms do you support?
We support Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Zoho Mail, and other IMAP based systems. Our email migration services are flexible and designed to support different business and technical requirements.
6. How much does email migration cost?
Cost depends on the number of users, platforms involved, and complexity of your environment. We offer transparent, project-based pricing with no hidden fees. Connect with our team for a detailed assessment and receive a clear quote for your migration.
7. Do you provide support after the migration is complete?
Yes. Post-migration support is included as standard. We monitor the new environment, resolve any issues, and deliver admin documentation so your IT team can manage the platform from day one.
