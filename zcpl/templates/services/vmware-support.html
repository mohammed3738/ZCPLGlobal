The Challenge

The Broadcom Acquisition Changed Everything

Since Broadcom acquired VMware in 2023, it ended perpetual licenses and support renewals, forcing customers toward expensive VCF subscriptions. This has driven annual costs up by 2–10 times, pushing many organisations to seek third-party support to maintain their perpetual licenses, strengthen existing VMware environments, or move away from Broadcom's services altogether.

For organisations seeking to retain their existing virtualisation investments, this represents a significant and sudden financial burden. Seeking an alternative VMware support model has become an essential consideration for teams looking to control costs without compromising service quality.

As a global leader of end-to-end third-party enterprise software support providers, Zaco Computers understands the importance of maintaining operational continuity and security for your existing VMware environment, without forcing costly upgrades or unnecessary licence bundles. Our vmware support is designed to protect what you have already built and paid for, at a fraction of the OEM cost.

📈

VMware License Cost Increases of 200–1000%

Broadcom ended perpetual licence renewals and restructured vmware software into mandatory VCF bundles. Organisations report vmware license cost increases of 2× to 10× their prior annual spend, with no phased pricing or transition period offered.

📦

Forced Product Bundling

VMware technical support under Broadcom now requires purchasing VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF), a bundled stack that includes vmware products many organisations do not need and did not budget for.

🔒

Perpetual Licences Left Without Coverage

Broadcom no longer offers support renewals for perpetual VMware licenses. Organisations that paid for and rely on vmware vsphere environments are now left without OEM-backed vmware technical support.

👥

Internal Skills Shortage

Managing vmware esxi hosts, vCenter, DRS, and HA configurations in-house requires deep expertise. Building and retaining that capability internally is costly, particularly when the environment is already stable and meeting operational needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

VMware Support: Common Questions

What is VMware third-party support and is it legal?
VMware third party support means engaging an independent provider rather than Broadcom to handle break/fix incidents, vulnerability advisories, and vmware technical support for your existing environment. It is entirely legal. Perpetual VMware licenses remain valid after Broadcom's changes; what ended was the option to purchase new perpetual licences and renew OEM support contracts.
Will our VMware environment still receive security coverage?
Yes. Our security and vulnerability management service actively monitors vmware software and vmware esxi host layers for known vulnerabilities, provides advisory on available patches, and implements mitigations, even where Broadcom is no longer issuing new patches for that ESXi or vSphere release.
Which versions of VMware vSphere and ESXi do you support?
We support organisations running legacy and current releases of vmware vsphere, including vmware esxi hypervisors and vCenter environments. During onboarding, our team assesses the specific version and configuration to ensure coverage aligns with the actual environment, including vmware linux-based host components.
Can you supply VMware licences as well as support?
Yes. We can procure and supply new vmware license and renewal licences for vSphere, Horizon, and Workstation directly, covering both vmware products licence procurement and ongoing vmware support from a single point of accountability.
How are SLAs structured? Can they be customised?
SLAs are fully customisable based on the criticality of each system and operational requirements. Differentiated response and resolution targets for production versus non-production environments are standard. Both annual and on-call engagement models are available; no single default arrangement is imposed on all clients.
Is it possible to migrate from VMware to Hyper-V or Proxmox without downtime?
Not zero downtime, but minimal. We build the target environment in parallel, migrate workloads in stages, and cut over during agreed maintenance windows. Business-critical systems move last, after validation on non-production workloads. The specific approach is confirmed during scoping based on the environment and downtime tolerance.
What is the minimum contract term?
We offer both annual managed it services agreements and on-call arrangements. Annual contracts provide predictable cost and priority SLAs for ongoing vmware managed services. On-call arrangements suit teams that need vmware technical support on an as-needed basis without a fixed annual commitment.
