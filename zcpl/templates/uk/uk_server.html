CERTIFIED REFURBISHED SERVER HARDWARE

What Sets Certified Refurbished Apart

The term "refurbished" is frequently misunderstood. At Zaco Computers UK Limited, certified refurbished servers are not simply second-hand equipment resold as-is. These are units procured in advance from OEMs, professionally stripped, inspected, repaired where necessary, deep-cleaned and tested against manufacturer standards before being certified for deployment.

For organisations facing End-of-Life (EOL) or End-of-Service-Life (EOSL) constraints, certified refurbished server hardware from Zaco provides a reliable, warranty-backed route to extending infrastructure without the capital outlay of new equipment. Every unit dispatched carries a 1-year warranty, extendable to suit your operational requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Enterprise IT Teams

Answers to the questions we receive most frequently from IT procurement leads and infrastructure managers buying new and refurbished servers. Contact us if your query is not addressed below.

1. Do refurbished servers come with a warranty?
- Yes, all refurbished servers supplied by Zaco Computers UK Limited include a warranty as standard. Most units carry a 1-year warranty, with options to upgrade to 2 or 3 years including onsite support. We are confident in our testing process and warranty everything we sell.
2. Can you configure a server to my exact specification?
- Absolutely. This is one of our key strengths. Tell us your required CPU model, RAM capacity, storage type and capacity, RAID configuration, networking cards, and any other requirements. Our engineers will build it from stock and test it before dispatch.
3. What is the difference between refurbished and used servers?
- A "used" server may be sold as-is with minimal inspection. A "refurbished" server at Zaco has been fully stripped, component-tested, cleaned, repaired where necessary, and rebuilt to a tested and certified standard — then supplied with a warranty. Our refurbished servers are not simply second-hand items.
4. Are refurbished servers reliable enough for business use?
- Yes, enterprise servers are designed for 24×7 operation for 5–8 years. Most refurbished servers we sell are 2–4 years old, meaning they have many years of reliable life remaining. Our thorough component-level testing and warranty backing provides additional assurance.
5. Do you supply servers to data centers and co-location facilities?
- Yes, bulk orders for data centres are a core part of our business. We offer competitive pricing for volume purchases, rack-ready configuration, and can manage staggered delivery schedules. Contact us with your project details for a tailored proposal.
6. Can i get support after purchasing a server from Zaco?
- Yes, we offer 24×7 technical remote support as part of our managed IT services. Our support desk can assist with installation queries, configuration, firmware issues, and warranty replacements. We also offer ongoing maintenance contracts for businesses that need ongoing server management.
