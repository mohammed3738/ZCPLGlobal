FAQ

Commonly Asked Questions About Enterprise Networking Solutions

1. What is an enterprise networking solution?
- An enterprise networking solution is a combination of networking hardware, software, and related technologies that enable secure, reliable, and high-performance connectivity across an organisation's IT infrastructure. It typically includes routers, switches, and network management systems used to support business operations.
2. Can you supply end-of-life (EOL) and legacy Cisco equipment?
- Yes, this is one of our key specialisms. We source EOL Cisco models including legacy Catalyst, ISR, ASR, and Nexus hardware no longer available through OEM channels.
3. What is the difference between Cisco Catalyst and Cisco Nexus switches?
- Cisco Catalyst switches are designed for enterprise LAN environments, covering campus access, distribution, and core switching for offices and buildings. Cisco Nexus switches are built for data centre environments, offering higher port density, low latency, and support for leaf-spine architectures.
