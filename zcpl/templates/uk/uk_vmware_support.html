FAQs

Commonly Asked Questions About VMware Third Party Support

1. Which VMware versions and products do you cover?
- We support legacy and current releases of VMware vSphere, including ESXi hypervisor and vCenter environments, as well as VMware Horizon and VMware Workstation. Coverage extends to older vSphere releases that Broadcom no longer supports under its standard agreements. During the onboarding process, our team assesses your specific version and configuration to confirm that coverage aligns precisely with your environment.
2. Do you provide migration services for UK organisations moving away from VMware?
- Yes. We support structured transitions from VMware vSphere to Hyper-V, from vSphere to Proxmox, and from on-premises environments to Azure managed services or hybrid cloud architectures. All migration activity is guided by your technical and business requirements, not by vendor preference. UK clients retain full control over the timeline and scope of any transition, and third-party support continues to cover your VMware environment throughout the process.
3. Can you supply VMware licences as well as provide support?
- Yes. We supply new and renewal VMware product licences alongside our managed IT services. This gives UK organisations a single accountable point of contact for both licensing and ongoing technical support, simplifying the vendor management and procurement process.
4. Will switching to third party support affect my existing VMware licences?
- No. Your perpetual VMware licences remain valid and unaffected. Third party support covers the maintenance and technical assistance around those licences. Nothing changes about licence ownership when you move away from Broadcom OEM support.
5. Do you cover older versions of VMware vSphere that Broadcom no longer supports?
- Yes. We support legacy and current vSphere releases including ESXi versions that have reached end of general support under Broadcom. Many UK businesses running stable older environments choose third party support specifically because it removes the pressure to upgrade before they are ready.
6. What is the process for starting VMware third-party support with Zaco?
- Email info@zacocomputer.com or call +44 20 3435 8060 with a brief overview of your VMware environment. We schedule a scoping call, carry out a free assessment, and provide a proposal with SLA options and pricing. Onboarding for most UK environments is completed within a few weeks of contract agreement.
